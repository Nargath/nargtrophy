# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Trophy banners: building pieces in their own hammer tab, always on. +15% damage dealt, -15% damage taken, 30 m radius.
- War standards: reusable items you plant at your feet, even mid-fight. +30% damage dealt, -25% damage taken, 12 m radius, 5 minutes, then a 15-minute cooldown.
- All 59 non-boss vanilla creatures that drop a trophy.
- Costs based on how easy each creature is to find and on its real trophy drop chance.
- 3 upgrade levels for both kinds, with a full refund when you deconstruct.
- Banners for modded creatures through `banners.<ModName>.yml` files, with suggested lines from `nt_trophies`.
- Server-synced banner files and gameplay settings.

Known limitations:

- Balance is a first pass.
- Deep North banners cost trophies only for now.
- Not yet tested on a dedicated server.
